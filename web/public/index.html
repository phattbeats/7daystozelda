Controls & guides

Turn your phone sideways. The pad appears when the game starts: left thumb stick move · A talk, open, confirm · B sword, cancel · Z Z-target · R shield · ▲▼◀▶ C-items · START pause menu · ESC settings.

Menus use the same pad: stick moves the cursor, A picks, B backs out, Z/R turn pages (keep turning until the arrows say To Workbench), START closes. Walk up to a placed workbench and press A to open it. Building: pick a kit on the Base tab, ◀/▶ rotate, A place, B cancel. On the pause menu you can also tap a slot to move the cursor, tap it again to pick, and swipe sideways to turn pages.

Pair over Bluetooth or plug in, then press any button once the game is up: browsers only reveal a controller after a press. On a phone the touch pad hides while a controller is connected and returns when it drops.

Defaults (Xbox names): left stick move · A confirm · B sword, cancel · LT Z-target · RT shield, next page · right stick C-items · LB L · Start pause · D-pad D-pad. PlayStation: ✕ is A, ○ is B, L2/R2 are the triggers. Some pads (Switch Pro, generic Bluetooth) report A/B swapped: rebind in Settings → Controller (ESC opens settings); it's remembered.

WASD move · X A · C B · Z Z-target · R R (shield, next page) · E L · Space Start (pause) · arrows C-items · TGFH D-pad · Tab Workbench · Esc settings menu. Rebind anything in Settings → Controller; it's remembered.
